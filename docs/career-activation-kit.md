# Harkingbade Career Activation Kit

Status: operating system ready; founder résumé evidence required  
Updated: 2026-08-16

## Objective

Use Harkingbade to create qualified interviews for one primary role family without presenting breadth as an unfocused list of skills.

The public category remains **Product & Growth Systems Builder**. Each application should translate that category into the employer's language and lead with one role hypothesis:

1. Growth Product
2. Product Marketing
3. Digital Product & Ecommerce
4. Zero-to-One Product Operator

Choose one anchor role for each 30-day cycle. Use adjacent skills as supporting evidence, not competing identities.

## Role-priority scorecard

Score every target role family `0–2` using approved founder evidence.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified outcomes | No approved result | Relevant responsibility, weak result | Auditable result in the role's language |
| Artifact depth | No artifact | Describable work | Inspectable public or private artifact |
| Recent relevance | Old/unclear | Transferable | Recent and directly relevant |
| Market demand | No target openings | Some plausible openings | Repeated openings in chosen market |
| Founder preference | Avoids core work | Neutral | Wants the daily work |
| Credibility gap | Major missing prerequisite | Learnable gap | Strong present fit |

Interpretation:

- `10–12`: primary role hypothesis.
- `7–9`: adjacent role; test selectively.
- `0–6`: do not lead with it this cycle.

Do not calculate this score until the résumé, project history, location/work authorization, and target market are supplied.

## Evidence inventory

Create one record for every relevant project, including work that cannot be public.

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Situation | Company/product context, customer, market, and timing |
| Consequence | Why the problem mattered commercially or operationally |
| Ownership | What the founder decided, implemented, coordinated, or influenced |
| Constraints | Time, budget, people, platform, compliance, or evidence limits |
| Decision | Alternatives considered and why one route was selected |
| Artifact | Brief, roadmap, research, copy, prototype, code, launch, dashboard, or system |
| Result | Metric, timeframe, baseline, comparison, and source |
| Confidence | Measured, documented, founder-reported, inferred, or unknown |
| Permission | Public, anonymized, interview-only, or private |

Use `docs/founder-evidence-intake.md` as the source worksheet. Never convert a responsibility into an outcome without evidence.

## Résumé bullet method

Use this structure:

```text
Decision/action + consequential object + operating context + verified result + measurement window
```

Working templates remain placeholders until evidence is supplied:

```text
[Repositioned / launched / improved] [product, offer, journey, or operating system]
for [customer/team context], resulting in [verified measure] over [window].
```

```text
Connected [research/product/marketing/engineering functions] around [decision],
reducing or increasing [verified outcome] from [baseline] to [result].
```

Delete adjectives that the artifact or result cannot prove.

## Application architecture

Every application gets four aligned layers:

1. **Headline:** exact target role plus the cross-functional advantage.
2. **First paragraph:** employer problem, relevant evidence, and why this mandate.
3. **Résumé:** only role-relevant bullets in the employer's language.
4. **Proof route:** one or two portfolio artifacts that validate the most consequential claim.

Do not send every Harkingbade page. Choose the shortest credible evidence path.

### Headline working templates

- Growth Product: `Growth Product operator connecting customer evidence, product decisions, activation, and measurable learning.`
- Product Marketing: `Product marketing operator connecting customer language, positioning, launch, adoption, and product delivery.`
- Digital Product & Ecommerce: `Digital product and ecommerce operator improving the journey from intent to conversion and retention.`
- Zero-to-One: `Zero-to-one product operator turning ambiguous opportunities into positioned, shipped, measurable systems.`

These are positioning templates, not claims of previous titles.

## Outreach templates

### Warm introduction request

```text
I am focusing on [role family] mandates where [specific cross-functional problem]
matters. My strongest relevant evidence is [approved result/artifact].

I noticed [company/team] is working on [specific context]. Would you be comfortable
introducing me to the person who owns [decision]? No pressure if the fit is unclear.
```

### Hiring-manager note

```text
Your [role/product context] appears to require more than [surface task]. The mandate
seems to connect [two or three functions] around [business/customer outcome].

I recently [approved, relevant evidence]. The short artifact is here: [one link].
If [specific constraint] is part of the role, I would value a focused conversation.
```

### Recruiter response

```text
Thanks. The strongest fit is a [role family] mandate owning [decisions/outcomes],
especially where product, positioning, delivery, and growth need to stay connected.

Before we schedule, could you share the mandate, reporting line, team shape,
location/work-authorization requirements, and the outcome expected in six months?
```

Personalize with public company context. Do not automate generic volume outreach.

## Interview proof story

Use the Harkingbade recovery as one transparent working case:

1. **Context:** a previously monetized broad publication reportedly fell from more than 100,000 monthly visits to effectively zero.
2. **Evidence boundary:** historical traffic remains founder-reported; the live domain outage and archive footprint are reproducible.
3. **Diagnosis:** availability failure and strategic-quality failure were separated.
4. **Decision:** replace search-volume dependence with a two-sided career/services system and evidence governance.
5. **Delivery:** Astro, Cloudflare Worker APIs, SEO controls, archive inventory, diagnostic, newsletter path, analytics gate, and rollback system.
6. **Tradeoffs:** no blanket redirects, fake freshness, invented founder claims, unapproved analytics collection, or premature advertising.
7. **Result:** a verified local commercial platform exists; live traffic, hiring, and revenue outcomes remain unproven until launch.
8. **Next decision:** account recovery, private evidence import, staging/cutover, and market validation.

This story demonstrates judgment only when uncertainty and unfinished outcomes remain visible.

## 30-day career sprint

### Week 1 — choose and package

- Supply the founder evidence intake and résumé.
- Score all four role families; select one anchor.
- Approve three outcome claims and two proof artifacts.
- Produce one role-specific résumé and one concise professional profile.

### Week 2 — calibrate

- Review twenty target openings in one geography/remote market.
- Extract repeated mandate language, prerequisites, outcomes, and team contexts.
- Hold four conversations with hiring managers, recruiters, or practitioners.
- Revise positioning only from repeated evidence.

### Week 3 — apply deliberately

- Submit five to eight high-fit applications.
- Request five relevant warm introductions.
- Send five evidence-led hiring-manager notes.
- Record proof route, response, objection, and stage.

### Week 4 — decide

- If conversations start but stall, improve proof and interview narrative.
- If applications receive no response, revisit role/market fit and first-page résumé language.
- If conversations reveal a prerequisite gap, decide whether to build evidence, narrow the role, or change the target.
- Do not increase volume until the failure point is understood.

## Decision metrics

Track weekly:

- qualified conversations by route;
- application-to-conversation rate;
- warm-introduction acceptance;
- hiring-manager response;
- screen-to-interview and interview-to-next-stage progression;
- repeated objections and missing proof;
- role fit, compensation band, work model, location, and mandate quality.

Raw application count is not a success measure.
